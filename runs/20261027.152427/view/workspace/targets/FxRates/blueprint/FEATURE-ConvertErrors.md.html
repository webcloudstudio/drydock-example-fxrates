FEATURE: Convert Errors

FieldValue
Version20261027 V1
DescriptionGET /convert refuses unknown currencies and missing publications with 404 and bad or missing inputs with 422.
Depends OnFEATURE-Convert.md
Providesconvert error contract
ConsumesGET /convert

Purpose

Fix the failure statuses of /convert.

Behavior

  • 404: a currency other than EUR with no rate on the effective date; no publication on or before the requested date; no publication stored at all.
  • 422 (FastAPI request validation): missing from, to, or amount; amount not matching [0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)?; a malformed or impossible date.
  • Error-response bodies are not part of the contract.

Programmatic Acceptance

=== AC convert-unknown-currency-404 ===
Intent: A currency with no stored rate on the effective date answers 404, whichever side it is on.
Requires: executable=python3; scope=test
Requires: python-package=fastapi; scope=test
Requires: python-package=httpx2; scope=test

from persistence.testing import db_session from app_server.testing import web_client

d = "2999-05-01"

with db_session() as db: db.rates.replace_rate(date=d, currency="ZZA", rate="1.5") client = web_client() to_unknown = client.get("/convert", params={"from": "ZZA", "to": "ZZX", "amount": "1", "date": d}) assert to_unknown.status_code == 404 from_unknown = client.get("/convert", params={"from": "ZZX", "to": "ZZA", "amount": "1", "date": d}) assert from_unknown.status_code == 404 === END AC convert-unknown-currency-404 ===

=== AC convert-validation-422 ===
Intent: Missing parameters, a malformed amount, and malformed or impossible dates are all refused with 422.
Requires: executable=python3; scope=test
Requires: python-package=fastapi; scope=test
Requires: python-package=httpx2; scope=test

from app_server.testing import web_client

client = web_client() base = {"from": "USD", "to": "GBP", "amount": "1"}

for missing in ["from", "to", "amount"]: params = {k: v for k, v in base.items() if k != missing} assert client.get("/convert", params=params).status_code == 422, missing

for bad_amount in ["abc", "-1", "1.", ".5", "1e3"]: params = dict(base, amount=bad_amount) assert client.get("/convert", params=params).status_code == 422, bad_amount

for bad_date in ["2025-13-01", "2025-01-31-x", "yesterday"]: params = dict(base, date=bad_date) assert client.get("/convert", params=params).status_code == 422, bad_date === END AC convert-validation-422 ===
