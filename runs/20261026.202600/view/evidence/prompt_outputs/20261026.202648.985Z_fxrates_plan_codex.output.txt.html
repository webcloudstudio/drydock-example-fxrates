kind:          capability

acceptance:    yes
instructions: |
  Implement GET /convert with required from, to, and amount parameters and an optional date.
  Validate amount against [0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)? and validate any supplied date as a real

  from the unrounded stored rates and amount, rounded half-even to two decimal places. Emit
  both values with fixed decimal places and echo the amount text unchanged.
